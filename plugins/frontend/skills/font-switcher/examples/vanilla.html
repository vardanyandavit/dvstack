font-switcher / vanilla

Pick a typeface.

Open the Aa button in the top corner. Every option previews in its own face, the page switches instantly, and the choice survives a reload with no flash of the wrong font.

Only what you use

On first load the browser downloads just the active font. The others are fetched when the picker opens, so five presets cost about the same as one until someone goes looking.

Pairs, not single fonts

Each preset sets a heading face (--font-display) and a body face (--font-sans). Style your page with those two variables and every preset just works.
